The Unleashed Folly Beach Bachelorette Party Guide

Folly Beach Guide From A Local For Bach Party

Planning a bachelorette party on Folly Beach? Let's get one thing straight: this isn't downtown Charleston, and it *definitely* isn't Miami. Folly is "The Edge of America," a funky, laid-back surf town where flip-flops are the required footwear, and the vibe is more about live music at a dive bar than velvet ropes at a club.

As a local, I'm here to give you the guide for 2025. We'll skip the tourist traps and national booking agencies and focus on the authentic Folly experience. This guide is built to help you plan the perfect weekend for your crew, centered around the beach, the boat, and the amazing local businesses that make this island special.

Why Folly? (The 2025 Vibe Check)

Before you book, let's make sure Folly is the right fit. The Charleston area has three main "zones," all with totally different personalities.

  • Downtown Charleston: This is for the "Uptown Girl" party. It's historic, cobblestone streets, upscale dining, and fancy rooftop bars. The nightlife is centered on Upper King Street. You'd choose this if your bride wants to wear heels, go to nice dinners, and explore history and shopping.
  • Isle of Palms / Sullivan's Island: This is the "Polished Resort" party. It's more family-oriented, upscale, and quiet. Think golf resorts (Wild Dunes) and pristine, wide beaches. It's beautiful, but the nightlife is almost non-existent. You'd choose this for a very relaxed, luxury-focused, spa-and-beach weekend.
  • Folly Beach: This is the "Funky, Laid-Back" party. Folly is a true-blue surf town. It's eclectic, a little gritty (in a good way), and 100% casual. The nightlife is a 5-block strip of dive bars, rooftop patios, and live music. You'll live in a swimsuit and flip-flops. You choose Folly if your bride wants to hang on a boat, bar-hop in a sun dress, and not take herself too seriously. With the **newly rebuilt Folly Beach Pier** as our stunning centerpiece, Folly is more ready than ever for a party.

If Folly sounds like your speed, keep reading. This guide is for you.

Where to Stay on Folly Beach for a Bachelorette Party

For a bachelorette party, your location is key. You want to be within walking or golf cart distance of Center Street. This is the island's "downtown," where every restaurant, bar, and shop is located. I recommend staying anywhere between 3 blocks East and 8 blocks West of Center Street.

Best Hotels for a Bachelorette Group

If you have a smaller group or just prefer hotel amenities, there are two fantastic options.

1. The Tides Hotel

This is the classic, no-brainer choice. It's the *only* true oceanfront hotel on Folly, located right at the new pier and directly on Center Street. You cannot beat this location. It has a great pool (a party scene in itself), a lively tiki bar (a must-stop), and you can walk to absolutely everything. It's the center of the action.

2. Regatta Inn

If you want something more boutique, quiet, and upscale, this is it. Located on the Folly River (the "back" side of the island), it's more of a luxury bed & breakfast. It's quieter, has stunning sunset views over the marsh, and is perfect for a smaller group or if the bride wants a more luxurious, spa-like escape. It's still an easy walk to all the action on Center Street.

Best House Rentals (VRBO & Local)

A house is the ultimate choice for a group. You get a kitchen for pre-gaming and group breakfasts, and a common area to hang out. When searching on VRBO, look for these keywords:

  • "Private Pool": In the summer, this is a game-changer.
  • "Oceanfront": The dream. You'll pay a premium, but waking up to the ocean is unbeatable.
  • "River View with Dock": This is a major pro-tip. A house on the river side often comes with a private or shared dock, which is perfect for being picked up *directly* by your private boat charter.

Local Rental Companies

If you're striking out on VRBO (many homes have Saturday-to-Saturday weekly rentals in peak summer), call a true local agency. They manage properties that aren't listed elsewhere and can help you find the perfect 3-4 night stay.

  • Fred Holland Realty: A Folly institution. They've been on the island forever and manage a huge number of properties.
  • Carolina One Vacations: Another major, trusted local company with a large inventory of homes.
  • StayDuvet: A more "boutique" rental company that often has properties with stylish, bachelorette-friendly decor.

The 2025 Folly Bachelorette "Local Business" Directory

This is the most important part of your planning. Booking with these *actual* local entities will make your party smoother, more authentic, and often cheaper than using a national middleman.

Category 1: The Essential Boat Day (Book This First!)

You cannot come to Folly and not get on the water. It's the #1 bachelorette activity. Do *not* book this through a third-party site. Call these local captains directly for a private, custom bachelorette charter. You can cruise the creeks, look for dolphins, and party at a sandbar.

Flipper Finders

A Folly Beach staple for decades. They run eco-tours and dolphin-watching trips, but their specialty is private bachelorette charters on comfortable, stable pontoon boats. They know the water better than anyone. BYOB.

SeaEO Luxury Boat Charters

Based just off the island, this company specializes in higher-end, private bachelorette and party cruises. If you want a bit more of a "luxury" feel for your boat day, they are an excellent choice. BYOB.

Tideline Tours

Another fantastic local option known for their eco-science and history tours, but they happily run private bachelorette parties. Their captains are incredibly knowledgeable. They can take you to the Morris Island Lighthouse or on a sandbar-hopping adventure.

Sail Folly

Want a different vibe? Sail Folly offers private charters on beautiful catamarans. It's less of a "floating party" and more of a relaxing, scenic, wind-in-your-hair experience. Perfect for a sunset cruise.

Category 2: The "Luxe" Beach Day (Zero Effort)

CRITICAL REMINDER: Alcohol is **NOT** allowed on Folly Beach. Cops are strict, and the fine is over $1,000. It's not worth the risk. The local solution is to...
Folly Beach Chair Company

This is the ultimate local life-hack. This amazing business will set up a full "bachelorette" lounge for your crew *before you even arrive*. Think comfy chairs, large umbrellas, cabanas, tables, and **coolers pre-filled with ice**—all waiting for you in the perfect spot (usually right by the pier). It's the best way to have a home base on the beach, and they handle all the heavy lifting. They also partner with other locals to offer **beach yoga**, **beach picnics**, and **surf lessons**.

Serenity Tree Yoga

Want to start one morning with some zen? Melora and her team at Serenity Tree are the local go-to for private bachelorette yoga sessions right on the beach. They will come to you at your rental or a public beach access for a relaxing, all-levels flow. A perfect, wholesome activity before a day of drinking.

Category 3: The "Stay-In" House Party Services

For one night, stay in and have the party come to your rental. It's often more fun and more affordable than a big night out.

Decor & Planning: Bach Babes / Charleston Bach Party

These are Charleston-based (but Folly-serving) party planners that will come to your Folly rental *before you arrive* and fully decorate it for your theme. You walk into a 100% party-ready house. They can also stock the fridge and set up gift bags.

Mobile Bartenders: Squeeze Cocktail & Bar Co.

Want craft cocktails without the Center Street crowd? Hire a mobile mixologist like Squeeze. They will come to your rental with a full bar setup and a professional bartender to craft delicious, custom cocktails for your group for a few hours. It feels incredibly luxurious.

Private Chefs: Let's Hibachi

This is a *wildly* popular bachelorette choice right now. This local company brings a full-size hibachi grill and a professional (and entertaining) chef *to your rental*. They set up on the porch or in the yard and cook for your entire group. It's dinner *and* a show, and everyone loves it.

Category 4: Party Transportation (The Necessity)

Golf Cart Rentals: Folly Beach Golf Cart Rentals / Salty's

If your house is more than 3-4 blocks from Center Street, a golf cart is NOT optional, it's essential. It's the standard way to get around the island. **You must book these MONTHS in advance.** They *will* sell out. Call a local company like Folly Beach Golf Carts or Salty's Golf Carts to reserve your 4- or 6-seater cart.

Category 5: Capture the Moments (Photographers)

Local Photographers: Oriana Photography / Emily Baucom

You're all dressed up, why not get professional photos? Hiring a local photographer for a 1-hour "mini-session" is a fantastic idea. They can meet you at the Morris Island Lighthouse for sunset, on the beach, or at the new pier. Oriana Photography and Emily Baucom are just two examples of many talented Charleston-area photographers who specialize in bachelorette and group shoots.

Where to Eat & Drink in 2025 (By Vibe)

Everything is on or just off Center Street. Here is your cheat sheet, expanded with local intel.

For the "Must-Have" Group Brunch

Lost Dog Cafe

Vibe: The most famous breakfast on Folly. A quirky, dog-themed institution.
Best For: A classic Folly experience.
Pro-Tip: They do *not* take reservations. Expect a 1-2 hour wait on a weekend morning. Go *very* early (before 9 AM) or put your name in and walk to the beach while you wait.

Rita's Seaside Grille

Vibe: Lively, open-air, upscale-casual American.
Best For: The "big group brunch" because **they take reservations!**
Pro-Tip: This is your best bet for a Sunday departure brunch. You can book a table, have a delicious, civilized meal (their brunch menu is huge), and not stress about a 2-hour wait.

For Casual "Come-as-You-Are" Lunch/Dinner

Taco Boy

Vibe: Funky, colorful, and always a party.
Best For: Your arrival-night dinner or a casual group lunch.
Pro-Tip: Their new, massive Folly location is perfect for a group. The tacos are great, but the margaritas (and the guac!) are the main event. They get crowded, but the space is huge so the line moves.

Chico Feo

Vibe: The absolute definition of Folly. It's a funky, cash-only (mostly) outdoor shack with a Caribbean-inspired menu.
Best For: A super-casual lunch or listening to live music at night.
Pro-Tip: This is a "locals" spot. Don't be in a hurry. Order the "Street Tacos" or the Cuban sandwich from the window, grab a beer, and find a seat in the yard. The vibe is immaculate.

The Lowlife Bar

Vibe: Your "cool" spot. It's an airy, retro-inspired bar with a fantastic shaded patio.
Best For: Craft cocktails and what might be the best burger on the beach.
Pro-Tip: This is a great first stop when you arrive. It's casual enough for a travel day but feels special. Their frozen drinks are top-notch.

Wiki Wiki Sandbar

Vibe: Tropical, tiki-inspired, and fun.
Best For: A rooftop cocktail and great Hawaiian-style food.
Pro-Tip: This is a fantastic group spot, especially the rooftop. It's a bit newer and brings a fun, tiki-bar energy. Great for photo ops with their colorful drinks.

For the "Big Night Out" Dinner

The Bounty Bar

Vibe: Craft cocktails in a "moody" but still-beachy setting.
Best For: The best "craft cocktails" on the island, with a creative, delicious dinner menu to match.
Pro-Tip: Located in the old (and tiny) Taco Boy spot, this is perfect for a slightly more elevated but still casual group dinner. The food is inventive and shareable.

Pier 101 Restaurant & Bar

Vibe: Casual, tourist-friendly, with unbeatable views.
Best For: A sunset drink *on* the new pier.
Pro-Tip: The food is standard beach fare (fried shrimp, burgers), but you're here for the location. It's the only restaurant literally *over* the ocean. Go for a pre-dinner drink and a group photo on the pier.

The Bar-Hopping Scene (A Must-Do)

1. The Tiki Bar at BLU (in The Tides)

Your first stop. It's oceanfront, at the base of the pier. You *must* get a "Folly's Finest" frozen drink (a Piña Colada/Rum Floater combo). It's the law. This is where you'll see every other bachelorette and bachelor party. Great energy.

2. Snapper Jack's

Your rooftop party. A three-story bar with a rooftop deck that has the best ocean views on Center Street. It gets packed and turns into a full-on party/dance floor at night. A classic Folly party spot.

3. The "Hidden" Revelry Brewing Bar

Your "in-the-know" stop. One of Charleston's best local breweries, Revelry, has a tiny, "hidden" beach bar *behind* Coconut Joe's. You have to walk down a small alley to find it. It's a must-find for craft beer and a slightly more chill vibe.

4. Sand Dollar Social Club

Your "real Folly" stop. Want the *real* Folly? End your night here. It's the island's best-loved, no-frills local dive bar. It's famous for its pool tables, cheap beer, and raucous karaoke. It's an experience every bachelorette party should have.

Sample 4-Day Folly Bachelorette Itinerary (2025)

Here’s how to put it all together for a perfect, stress-free weekend.

Thursday (Arrival Day)

  • 3:00 PM: Arrive at CHS, grab your bags, and head to Folly. Stop for groceries/booze on the way (there's a Publix on Folly Road).
  • 5:00 PM: Check into your rental house (which is already decorated by **Bach Babes**!). Settle in.
  • 6:30 PM: First drinks! Walk to **The Lowlife Bar** for a celebratory frozen cocktail and an amazing burger.
  • 8:30 PM: Head back to the house for the Lingerie Party, gift opening, and games.

Friday (Beach & Bar Hop)

  • 10:00 AM: Morning recovery with **Serenity Tree Yoga** on the beach.
  • 12:00 PM: Head to your pre-set **Folly Beach Chair Company** beach lounge. Relax, swim, and play games for the afternoon. Order sandwiches to be delivered to the beach.
  • 4:30 PM: Head back to the house to shower and get ready.
  • 6:00 PM: Sunset drinks. Walk to **Pier 101** for a group photo and a drink over the ocean.
  • 7:30 PM: "Big Night Out" Dinner. Walk to **The Bounty Bar** for craft cocktails and a fantastic group meal.
  • 9:30 PM: The Folly Bar Hop begins! Hit the **Tiki Bar at BLU**, then the rooftop at **Snapper Jack's**, find the **Revelry** bar, and end the night with karaoke at the **Sand Dollar**.

Saturday (The Big Boat Day)

  • 9:30 AM: A (potentially rough) morning. Walk to **Lost Dog Cafe**. Put your name in, grab a coffee, and wait. It's worth it.
  • 11:30 AM: Head to the dock (maybe your rental's!) to meet your charter.
  • 12:00 PM - 4:00 PM: **Private Bachelorette Boat Party!** Your charter from **Flipper Finders** takes you cruising the creeks, looking for dolphins, and floating at a sandbar. This will be the highlight of your trip.
  • 4:30 PM: Back at the house, exhausted and happy. Relax, nap, and get ready for the "night in."
  • 7:00 PM: The party comes to you. The **Let's Hibachi** private chef arrives at your rental for a hilarious, fun, and delicious dinner. The **Squeeze Cocktail** mobile bartender is there serving up drinks. It's the perfect, stress-free final night.

Sunday (Departure)

  • 9:00 AM: Pack up the house.
  • 10:30 AM: One last group meal. Head to **Rita's Seaside Grille** for your 10:30 AM reservation. Have a great brunch and one final mimosa.
  • 12:00 PM: Grab your bags and head to the airport.

Folly Beach Bachelorette FAQs (The Must-Knows)

NO. 100% SERIOUSLY. This is the #1 rule. No alcohol (not even in a cup) is allowed on the sand. The fine is over $1,000, and they *will* ticket you. Folly is not a "slap on the wrist" place. Be smart. Don't be obvious. If you *must* have a drink, put it in an unmarked tumbler (like a Yeti) and be *extremely* discreet. Do not bring cans or bottles. Your best bet is to just drink at the beach bars or on your boat.

It's the worst. This is why you must rent a house with dedicated parking and use golf carts. When parking on the street (if you can find a spot), *all four tires* must be completely off the pavement, and you must park *with* the flow of traffic. They are ruthless with tickets. Read every sign.

There is one grocery store on the island: **Bert's Market**. It's a 24/7 local legend. It has a surprisingly good selection of items, a great deli, and beer/wine. For a *big* stock-up (cases of water, seltzers, etc.), stop at the **Publix on Folly Road** (on James Island) *before* you get to the beach. You can also use Instacart, which will deliver from this Publix.

Shoulder season: April-May or September-October. The weather is perfect (80s), it's less crowded, and rental prices are lower. Peak summer (June-August) is great but it is extremely hot (95+ with 100% humidity), very crowded, and rental prices are at their highest.

What to Wear (And What to Leave at Home)

The Vibe: Folly is "boho-surfer-casual." You will look very out of place in club-wear and stilettos. Think flowy dresses, high-end denim shorts, cute matching sets, sandals, and wedges. It's a stylish-but-casual crowd.

Leave at Home:

  • ✖ High heels (You'll sink in the sand)
  • ✖ Anything super formal or "clubby"
  • ✖ Any clothes you'd be sad to get salt water on

Must-Pack List:

  • ✔ Themed swimsuits for the boat day
  • ✔ A white outfit (or three) for the bride
  • ✔ A nice (but casual) "flowy" dress for your big dinner
  • ✔ Denim shorts and cute tops
  • ✔ Flat sandals and/or wedges
  • ✔ A hat and sunglasses (non-negotiable)
  • ✔ **Lots and lots of sunscreen**
