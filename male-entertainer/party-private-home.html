Schedule Michael's performance during reasonable evening hours, typically between 7-10 PM. This timing respects neighbors while still allowing your celebration to feel like an evening event. Sound Management

Michael uses professional sound equipment with adjustable volume. Respecting Quiet Hours

Most neighborhoods have informal or formal quiet hours (typically 10 or 11 PM). Plan your entertainment schedule so Michael's performance concludes before these hours. Your celebration can continue afterward at lower volumes, but the high-energy entertainment portion should respect neighborhood standards.
