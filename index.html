When to Go

Spring (March–May) and fall (September–November) are peak season. Summer is best for beach and boat trips; winter means lower prices and easier reservations. Book April, May, and October weekends early.

Where to Stay

Downtown puts you walking distance from the bars: The Dewberry, Hotel Bennett, The Restoration, The Ryder, The Charleston Place, and Zero George. The French Quarter is quieter, with The Vendue, Hotel Emeline, French Quarter Inn, The Spectator Hotel, and Wentworth Mansion. Mount Pleasant has Charleston Harbor Resort and Old Village Post House, and for a beach house look at Tides Folly Beach, Wild Dunes Resort, Island Realty, Vrbo, or Airbnb.

Rooftop Bars

Start the night up high. The original Charleston rooftop is The Rooftop at The Vendue in the French Quarter, with views over the harbor, while Stars Rooftop & Grill Room looks out over Upper King in every direction. For something more upscale, Citrus Club at The Dewberry sits eight floors above Marion Square, and The Cocktail Club pairs serious craft cocktails with a rooftop terrace and private rooms that suit a bachelorette group. Near the Ravenel Bridge, Revelry Brewing has a rooftop made for sunset beers.

King Street Nightlife and a Charleston SC Pub Crawl

Once the sun goes down, Prohibition turns its Upper King dining room into a dance floor on weekend nights. For a slower start, try the pre-Prohibition cocktails at The Gin Joint on East Bay or a round of wine and cheese at Bin 152 in the French Quarter. If you'd rather someone else plan the route, the Upper King Street Cocktail Tour walks your group through three popular bars, and live music fans can catch a show on the deck at Charleston Pour House on James Island.

Brunch

Brunch is non-negotiable. Poogan’s Porch serves Lowcountry classics in a Victorian house downtown, and Toast! handles big groups without fuss. Reserve early for the Sunday Gospel Brunch at Halls Chophouse, or catch live jazz over weekend brunch at High Cotton on East Bay. Magnolias and Slightly North of Broad keep things polished and Southern, Millers All Day serves breakfast whenever you roll in, and the morning after calls for a box from Callie’s Hot Little Biscuit or a farm-driven plate with a Middle Eastern twist at Butcher & Bee.

Group Dinners Downtown

For the big group dinner, Husk serves Southern ingredients in a restored Queen Street mansion, and FIG runs a seasonal menu that books up fast. Seafood lovers can split oysters at 167 Raw on East Bay or the bright raw bar at The Darling Oyster Bar on King. Leon’s Oyster Shop pairs fried chicken with frozé, and Rodney Scott’s BBQ does whole-hog barbecue on upper King.

Boat Tours and Water Activities

Get on the water at least once. Schooner Pride sails a tall ship through the harbor at sunset, and Spiritline Cruises and Charleston Harbor Tours run harbor cruises, dinner cruises, and group charters. For something rowdier, Saltwater Cycle runs a BYOB pedal-powered party boat and a tiki barge, while Coastal Expeditions leads kayak and paddleboard trips around Shem Creek and the barrier islands.

Folly Beach, Sullivan's Island, Isle of Palms, and Shem Creek

On Folly Beach, Rita’s Seaside Grille has a patio steps from the pier. Sullivan's Island has burgers on Middle Street at Poe’s Tavern, brunch and a lively bar at The Obstinate Daughter, and barbecue with live music at Home Team BBQ. On Isle of Palms, The Windjammer is the beachfront bar with a stage. Back in Mount Pleasant, Shem Creek is lined with deck bars: Red’s Icehouse for sunset drinks, Tavern & Table for a waterfront meal, and Shem Creek Bar & Grill for casual seafood on the dock.

Tastings and Day Trips

Make a day trip to Wadmalaw Island for tea at Charleston Tea Garden and a tasting at Deep Water Vineyard. Closer in, Firefly Distillery in North Charleston is home to sweet tea vodka, High Wire Distilling runs tours on Upper King, and beer drinkers can split the afternoon between Holy City Brewing, Westbrook Brewing in Mount Pleasant, and the brewpub at Edmund’s Oast.

Workshops and Spa

For a low-key Charleston bachelorette idea that leaves everyone with a keepsake, book a BYOB candle-making class at Candlefish, where everyone pours their own scented candles. Recover with group treatments at The Spa at Charleston Place, and check Explore Charleston for events happening during your weekend.
