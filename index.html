Charleston Bachelorette Party Guide

Your Complete Checklist for the Ultimate Girls Weekend in Charleston, SC

Southern charm meets unforgettable nights out

Charleston is the ultimate girls trip destination, combining Southern charm, stunning beaches, award-winning restaurants, and a vibrant nightlife scene. Whether you're planning a bachelorette party, celebrating a milestone birthday, or organizing a girls' weekend escape, Charleston delivers the perfect mix of relaxation and excitement.

From historic downtown streets lined with pastel-colored houses to rooftop bars with harbor views, Charleston offers something for every type of celebration. The walkable downtown means you can bar crawl, shop, and dine without needing a car. Plus, beautiful beaches are just 15-20 minutes away when you need that beach day vibe.

Planning a bachelorette party with guests under 21? Charleston offers fantastic stay-in party options that keep the celebration private and fun without the bar scene. Consider booking a beach house rental in Isle of Palms or Folly Beach where you can host pool parties, arrange private entertainment, enjoy catered dinners, and create unforgettable memories without age restrictions. A stay-in bachelorette party gives you complete control over the vibe, music, and activities while avoiding the hassle of mixed-age groups trying to navigate Charleston's 21+ nightlife venues.

Things to Do in Charleston

Beyond the bars — Charleston offers unique activities perfect for your bachelorette itinerary

🏖️ Beach Days

Folly Beach offers a laid-back surfer vibe with beach bars and rental shops. Sullivan's Island provides quieter stretches perfect for relaxing. Isle of Palms features family-friendly atmosphere with plenty of beach amenities and restaurants.

🛥️ Boat Tours & Water Activities

Charleston Harbor boat tours showcase the city from the water. Sunset cruises with cocktails create Instagram-worthy moments. Kayak tours through marshlands, paddleboarding, and party pontoon boat rentals available for adventurous groups.

🛍️ Shopping on King Street

Upper King Street boutiques offer trendy fashion finds. Lower King Street features high-end shops and local designers. Pick up matching bachelorette party outfits, gifts for the bride, and Charleston souvenirs during your shopping spree.

🧖‍♀️ Spa & Wellness

Charleston Day Spa offers group packages and champagne. Earthling Day Spa provides organic treatments downtown. Spa at Belmond Charleston Place delivers luxury experiences. Perfect for recovering from last night's festivities.

📸 Instagram Photo Spots

Rainbow Row's colorful historic houses are Charleston's most photographed location. Pineapple Fountain in Waterfront Park, The Battery's waterfront views, and Angel Oak Tree provide stunning backdrops for your bachelorette photos.

🎨 Unique Experiences

Paint and sip classes with wine at Bottle + Bottega. Ghost tours through Charleston's haunted historic district. Charleston Culinary Tours combine food tastings with history. Cooking classes at Charleston Cooks teach Lowcountry cuisine.
