Things to Do for a Charleston Bachelorette Party

Looking for Charleston bachelorette party ideas that go beyond dinner and drinks? Plan a Charleston bach party with a harbor boat tour, a funny improv show at Theatre 99, a group paint session or a relaxing spa visit. These Charleston bachelorette activities work for different group sizes and help you mix daytime experiences with a fun night out.

For an easy Charleston bachelorette party itinerary, start with a food tour or Folly Beach day trip, enjoy rooftop cocktails around King Street, then head to dinner and dancing. Whether you're planning a full weekend or one afternoon, the Charleston bachelorette party activities below give you flexible options. Check each business for availability and group bookings before finalizing your plans.
