Where to Stay in Downtown Charleston for a Bachelorette Party

Airbnb rentals, downtown neighborhoods and the best hotels near King Street.

Planning a charleston bachelorette weekend? Choosing where to stay is one of the biggest decisions for your group. Downtown Charleston has plenty of restaurants, bars, shopping and nightlife, but location can make a huge difference.

Most bachelorette groups start by looking for an Airbnb or other vacation rental, if available. Having an entire home makes it easier to split costs, get ready together and spend time as a group. If a suitable rental isn't available, downtown hotels are an excellent alternative.

Best overall recommendation: Look for a legal vacation rental near Upper King Street or Cannonborough–Elliotborough. If you prefer a hotel, stay around King Street, Marion Square or the Historic District.

1. Airbnb and Vacation Rentals in Downtown Charleston

An Airbnb is often the most practical choice for a larger bachelorette group. Many rentals offer multiple bedrooms, shared living rooms, kitchens and more privacy than separate hotel rooms.

Start searching early, especially for spring and fall weekends. Downtown short-term rentals are regulated, so availability may be limited.

2. Best Downtown Charleston Neighborhoods

Upper King Street

Best for nightlife. Upper King Street is ideal for groups that want to be close to popular bars, restaurants, music and late-night entertainment. It is one of the most convenient downtown areas for a bachelorette weekend.

Cannonborough–Elliotborough

Best for group rentals. These neighborhoods are just off Upper King Street. You will find historic homes, coffee shops, restaurants and some vacation rentals. Check walking distances before booking.

Historic District and French Quarter

Best for sightseeing and restaurants. This area features classic Charleston architecture, historic streets, shopping and upscale hotels. It is ideal for groups wanting a more relaxed, walkable Charleston experience.

Marion Square

Best central location. Staying near Marion Square puts your group between Upper King nightlife and the Historic District. It is a great compromise for a mixed itinerary.

3. Downtown Charleston Hotels for Bachelorette Groups

Hotels are a good alternative if you cannot find a legal vacation rental or prefer amenities such as housekeeping, restaurants and front desk service.

  • The Ryder Hotel Boutique hotel near King Street with a social atmosphere and poolside bar.
  • The Mills House Historic pink hotel on Meeting Street with a pool and easy access to downtown attractions.
  • Hyatt House Charleston Historic District Suite-style accommodations on Upper King Street.
  • Hotel Bennett Luxury hotel overlooking Marion Square.
  • Francis Marion Hotel Historic King Street hotel in a convenient central location.
  • The Restoration Charleston Residential-style suites near King Street.
  • The Charleston Place Full-service luxury hotel in the Historic District.

4. Tips for Booking a Charleston Bachelorette Stay

  • Book early: Spring and fall weekends are popular.
  • Count beds: Sleeping 10 does not always mean 10 separate beds.
  • Check bathrooms: Multiple bathrooms make getting ready easier.
  • Check parking: Downtown parking can add to your total cost.
  • Compare fees: Review taxes, cleaning fees and hotel charges.
  • Check the map: Not every Charleston listing is actually downtown.

If your group is driving, read our free parking in downtown Charleston guide before arriving.

5. Where Should Your Group Stay?

For most groups, a permitted Airbnb or vacation rental near Upper King Street is worth checking first. If that doesn't work, choose a downtown hotel near King Street or Marion Square.

Prioritize a good location, enough bathrooms, clear guest rules and a total price everyone agrees on.
