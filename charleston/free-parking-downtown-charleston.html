Where Is Free Parking in Downtown Charleston, SC?

Free street parking, Sundays, city lots and downtown parking tips.

Looking for free parking in downtown Charleston, SC? The easiest opportunities are city parking meters on Sundays, official city holidays and outside paid meter hours. Some city-operated surface lots also have free hours.

Quick Answer: City of Charleston street meters generally require payment Monday through Saturday from 9 AM to 6 PM. Meter payment is generally not required after 6 PM, before 9 AM, on Sundays or on official city holidays. Other posted restrictions still apply.

1. Free Street Parking After 6 PM

One of the easiest ways to avoid parking fees is to visit downtown Charleston in the evening. City-operated street meters generally stop charging at 6 PM and begin charging again at 9 AM on the next paid parking day.

This can be useful for evening dining, sightseeing and visiting downtown bars. However, always check signs before leaving your vehicle.

See the City of Charleston parking meter information .

Important King Street Restriction

Parking on King Street between Spring Street and John Street is prohibited from 6 PM to 3 AM Thursday, Friday and Saturday. Free meter hours do not override this restriction.

2. Free Parking Downtown on Sundays

Sunday is one of the best days to find free street parking downtown. City-operated street meters do not charge on Sundays.

You can look for legal metered spaces around downtown, including portions of the Historic District and streets near King Street.

Remember that residential permit restrictions, loading zones, temporary closures and other posted rules still apply.

3. Free Parking on Official City Holidays

City-operated street meters are also free on official City of Charleston parking holidays. Check the city's published rules before visiting, because not every federal or state holiday necessarily follows the same schedule.

Charleston Official Parking Information

4. Downtown Charleston City Surface Lots

Certain city-operated metered surface lots have the same general paid hours as street meters. They may be free outside those hours, on Sundays and on applicable official city holidays.

These are surface lots, not parking garages. Some have time limits even when no payment is required. Confirm the rules posted at each lot.

5. Is There Free Parking Near King Street?

Yes, but the time of day matters. Legal city-operated metered spaces near King Street may be free in the evening or on Sundays.

Upper King Street is a popular nightlife district, so parking can be difficult on busy weekends. Pay attention to the Thursday through Saturday evening parking restriction between Spring and John streets.

6. Free Parking Near Charleston City Market

The Charleston City Market is surrounded by busy downtown streets. You may find city metered parking nearby during free parking hours.

However, most nearby garages and private parking facilities charge their own fees. Do not assume garages become free on Sundays.

7. Free Downtown DASH Shuttle

Another way to save money is to park legally and use Charleston's free downtown DASH shuttle.

The shuttle is operated by CARTA and serves popular downtown areas. The ride is free, but any parking fees where you leave your vehicle are separate.

8. What About Downtown Parking Garages?

Parking garages in downtown Charleston generally charge fees even when street meters are free. If you cannot find street parking, a city garage may be a practical alternative.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is downtown Charleston parking free on Sunday?

City-operated street meters are free on Sundays. Some city metered surface lots are also free. Private lots and garages may still charge.

Is downtown Charleston street parking free after 6 PM?

Generally yes, for city street meters. Always follow posted restrictions, including special King Street rules.

Can you park overnight for free in Charleston?

Some legal street spaces do not require overnight meter payment, but time limits, residential restrictions and other posted rules can still apply.

Is King Street parking free?

Some city metered spaces may be free outside paid hours, but King Street has special parking restrictions on Thursday, Friday and Saturday nights.
