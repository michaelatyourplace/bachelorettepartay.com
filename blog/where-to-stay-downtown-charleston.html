Where to Stay in Downtown Charleston for a Bachelorette Party

Airbnbs, group rentals, boutique hotels, and the best areas near King Street.

Planning a bachelorette party weekend in Charleston? Where you stay makes a big difference, especially if the group plans to enjoy downtown restaurants, brunch, shopping, and nightlife. For most groups, the first place to look is an Airbnb or other whole-home rental, if available. A shared house gives everyone room to get ready and spend time together. But downtown Charleston also has excellent hotels and spacious suites.

Quick recommendation: Start by looking for a legal group rental near Upper King Street or Cannonborough–Elliotborough. If nothing suitable is available, book a hotel around King Street, Marion Square, or the Historic District.

1. Airbnb and Vacation Rentals: Best for Groups

Many bachelorette groups prefer an Airbnb because they can split the cost, use a shared living room, prepare breakfast, and get ready together before heading out. Large rentals near Upper King Street tend to be especially convenient for a weekend centered on restaurants and bars.

Important: Downtown short-term rentals are regulated and availability is limited. Before paying, confirm that the property is properly permitted, allows your full guest count, and permits the kind of gathering you have planned. Check the City of Charleston's short-term rental information. Never assume that a rental allows parties or outside visitors.

2. Best Downtown Neighborhoods for a Bachelorette Stay

Upper King Street

Best for nightlife. This is a strong home base if the group wants restaurants, bars, and evening entertainment nearby. Look around Upper King and the streets leading toward Marion Square. Walkability varies by the exact address.

Cannonborough–Elliotborough

Best for finding group houses. Just off Upper King, these connected neighborhoods have a mix of historic homes, restaurants, cafes, and vacation rentals. Check how far a listing is from King Street rather than relying on “downtown” in the title.

Historic District and French Quarter

Best for a classic Charleston weekend. Stay here for beautiful streets, sightseeing, restaurants, and upscale hotels. It's especially convenient for groups planning shopping, photos, sightseeing, and a more relaxed itinerary, although the busiest Upper King nightlife is farther away.

Near Marion Square

Best all-around location. The area around Marion Square sits between Upper King and the lower Historic District, making it a practical compromise for groups that want a little of everything.

3. Downtown Charleston Hotels Worth Checking

If a suitable Airbnb is unavailable—or the group simply prefers hotel service—these downtown hotels are worth comparing. Ask about adjoining rooms, suites, group blocks, parking, and total fees.

  • The Ryder Hotel A social, boutique-style option near King Street, with a poolside bar and bachelorette getaway offerings.
  • The Mills House The iconic pink hotel on Meeting Street. A photogenic Historic District choice with a pool and bachelorette package options.
  • Hyatt House Charleston / Historic District Suite-style accommodations on Upper King Street; worth checking when the group wants a little more space.
  • Hotel Bennett Luxury accommodations at Marion Square, well placed for an upscale weekend.
  • Francis Marion Hotel A historic King Street hotel overlooking Marion Square, handy for shopping and dining.
  • The Restoration Charleston Residential-style suites near King Street, useful for groups that want a more apartment-like hotel stay.
  • The Charleston Place A full-service luxury hotel in the heart of the Historic District.

For more options, browse Explore Charleston's downtown hotel listings.

4. Booking Tips Before You Split the Cost

  • Count actual beds. A listing that sleeps 10 may rely on sofa beds or shared beds.
  • Check bathrooms. Multiple bathrooms make getting ready much easier.
  • Compare the total price. Include cleaning fees, taxes, hotel fees, and parking before dividing the cost.
  • Look at the map. A property described as “Charleston” may be outside the downtown peninsula.
  • Read the rules. Check quiet hours, visitor policies, occupancy limits, and cancellation terms.
  • Book early. Popular spring and fall weekends can limit the best options for larger groups.

5. So, Where Should Your Group Stay?

For a typical bachelorette weekend, a permitted Airbnb or whole-home rental near Upper King Street is the first option worth checking. It gives the group a shared place to hang out and often makes the weekend easier to coordinate. If you can't find a good rental, a downtown hotel around King Street or Marion Square is a convenient alternative.

The biggest tip: prioritize location, enough bathrooms, and clear guest rules over a pretty listing photo. Your group will spend plenty of time exploring Charleston anyway.
