Charleston Bachelorette Party Guide for Under 21 Crowds

Planning a bachelorette party in Charleston with party members under 21? You're not alone!

Let's face it—not everyone in your bachelorette crew has hit 21 yet, but that doesn't mean Charleston can't deliver an unforgettable celebration. While most bars and clubs are strictly 21+, Charleston offers plenty of ways to party, bond, and make memories without the booze being the centerpiece.

Whether you're the bride-to-be with younger bridesmaids or you're planning a party for someone who's tying the knot young, Charleston's charm extends beyond the bar scene. From beach days to private entertainment, here's how to throw an epic bachelorette party that everyone can enjoy—regardless of age.

Top Things to Do for Under 21 Bachelorette Parties in Charleston

1. Beach Day at Folly or Sullivan's Island

Rent a beach house, bring matching swimsuits, set up games, and celebrate with a sunset photo shoot. All ages welcome, and it's free!

2. Private Boat or Harbor Tour

Book a private boat charter for dolphin watching, harbor cruising, and sunset views. Perfect for group bonding without age restrictions.

3. Spa Day & Pamper Sessions

Book group spa treatments, mani-pedis, or bring a mobile spa service to your Airbnb. Relaxation and girl time without the club scene.

4. Private Dinner & Restaurant Hopping

Make reservations at top Charleston restaurants. Many have private dining rooms perfect for bachelorette celebrations—all ages welcome during dinner service.

5. Historic Downtown Walking Tour

Explore Charleston's historic district with a private guide or self-guided tour. Take Instagram-worthy photos in Rainbow Row and Battery Park.

6. Rent a Party House for Private Celebration

Book a Charleston single house or downtown Airbnb. Host your own party with music, games, and catered food—no age limits, total control.

7. Music Farm 18+ Concert Night

If there's an 18+ show during your trip, grab tickets! It's the only traditional club option where everyone can get in and dance the night away.
